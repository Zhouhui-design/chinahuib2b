```
SeaHeart Global operates x2xhub.com, a global B2B online exhibition platform. The platform replaces physical trade shows with always-on virtual exhibition booths, allowing manufacturers to exhibit product lines to international buyers 24/7 without travel.

Exhibitors create booth pages featuring products with full specifications, verified company profiles, business certifications, and direct contact channels. Buyers browse exhibitions free of charge, communicate with suppliers through built-in real-time chat, and post sourcing tasks in the trade marketplace.

The platform interface supports 13 languages: English, Chinese, German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Russian, Portuguese, Hindi, Thai, and Vietnamese. Supplier verification covers business registration, industry certifications (ISO, CE, and others), and factory information.

Current exhibitor categories include CNC tooling, smart locks, security hardware, and custom metal manufacturing.
```
